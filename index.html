Research Profile

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and bioinformatics, with a focus on developing computational approaches to address complex biological problems. I am particularly interested in integrating biological foundation models, graph neural networks, and multi-omics data to uncover biological patterns, predict molecular interactions, and improve our understanding of complex biological systems. My broader research aims to bridge computational innovation and biological discovery, with potential applications in microbiology, biotechnology, and precision medicine.
